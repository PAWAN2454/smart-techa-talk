About Unique Pvt Ltd

We build intelligent voice-controlled robots for the future of automation.

Our Story

Unique Pvt Ltd was started with a vision to make robotics simple, smart, and voice-driven. Our flagship product, Smart Techa Talk Robo, is designed to understand and follow natural voice commands in English, Hindi, and Marathi.

Our Mission

To make intelligent automation accessible for education, industry, and everyday life.

Our Vision

To be India’s leading innovator in voice-enabled robotic systems that serve real-world needs.
